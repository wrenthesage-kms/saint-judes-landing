THE PEOPLE WHO LIVE HERE

DEMI-HUMANS

They are not monsters.

They are people whose biology exists somewhere inside the same deeply fucked history that produced humans, Behemoths, monsters, and everything else SJL has managed to accumulate over the last nineteen hundred years.

Some have fur. Some have scales. Some have wings. Some have tails. Some have anatomy that makes a doctor stare at their paperwork for a very long time.

NONE OF THAT MAKES THEM MONSTERS.

01 THE IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

"Demi-human" is a biological category. "Monster" is an ecological category. They overlap in ancestry, environment, and occasionally behavior, but they are not interchangeable.

BIOLOGY

EVERYTHING GOT COMPLICATED

Nobody in SJL gets to pretend biology stayed clean.

Humans existed first. Behemoths arrived later. Monsters came out of the resulting ecological nightmare. Somewhere across all that time, species fucked each other, adapted to each other, mutated, interbred, survived things they should not have survived, and kept having children.

The result is a population of people whose bodies can look wildly different from baseline humans while still being recognizably, socially, legally, and biologically people.

01

HUMANS

Baseline humanity. Still here. Still having children. Still insisting they understand what happened to the rest of biology.

02

BEHEMOTHS

Enormous biologically inconsistent creatures with anatomy, chemistry, and physical properties that do not behave the way ordinary life is supposed to behave.

03

MONSTERS

A broad ecological category covering predators, parasites, supernatural entities, mutants, mimics, wildlife, glitches, infestations, and things that should not technically exist.

04

DEMI-HUMANS

People. Their bodies may carry traits associated with animals, monsters, Behemoths, or stranger biological histories, but their existence is not defined by being a monster.

DO NOT FUCK THIS UP ANIMAL FEATURES ≠ MONSTER.

A person with bat ears is not automatically a bat monster. A person with scales is not automatically a reptile monster. A person with wings is not automatically something you should hunt with a shotgun.

THE BODY DOES WHATEVER IT WANTS

THERE IS NO STANDARD DEMI-HUMAN BODY

There are patterns. There are inherited traits. There are species lines and populations with recognizable physical characteristics. There are also exceptions because SJL has never respected anybody's tidy little classification system.

01

ANIMAL TRAITS

Ears, tails, fur, scales, claws, horns, wings, specialized teeth, unusual eyes, altered limbs, sensory adaptations, and other inherited physical traits are common.

02

SPECIES VARY

Two demi-humans from completely different populations may have almost nothing in common physically. "Demi-human" does not mean one species.

03

FAMILY TRAITS

Traits can run through families, communities, and regional populations. Some things are inherited predictably. Some are less predictable.

04

MONSTER TRAITS HAPPEN

A demi-human can have traits associated with a monster without becoming a monster. SJL biology is not a neat little flowchart.

05

THEY ARE STILL PEOPLE

They have jobs. Families. Homes. Religion or no religion. Bad landlords. Favorite restaurants. Cousins they cannot stand. Opinions about the city. Entirely ordinary problems.

SOCIETY

THIS IS NOT A SPECIES ZOO

Demi-humans are not one isolated population living in a designated fantasy quarter. They are part of SJL.

They live throughout the city. They work alongside humans. They own businesses. They raise children. They move between districts. They participate in the same cultural mess everybody else does.

Some communities maintain older traditions. Some are deeply urban. Some live in ways shaped by their species' biology. Some could not care less about any of that.

LANGUAGE

SJL is a cultural melting pot, and demi-human communities are part of that mess. English, Tagalog, Chinese, Japanese, and other languages coexist with local slang, family vocabulary, borrowed words, and whatever somebody's grandparents brought with them.

FAMILY

Family structures vary wildly. Some communities are tightly intergenerational. Some are scattered across districts. Some families have spent centuries living in the same neighborhood.

WORK

Demi-humans work everywhere. Industry, retail, transportation, construction, food, education, medicine, entertainment, scavenging, trades, government, crime, art, and everything else a city needs done.

SEX & REPRODUCTION

Reproduction between humans, demi-humans, monsters, and other biologically compatible beings is not treated as some impossible forbidden concept. It is part of the world's actual biology.

PEOPLE HAVE BEEN FUCKING EACH OTHER FOR ~1,900 YEARS.

Obviously the resulting biology is complicated.

PLACE MATTERS

WHERE YOU LIVE CHANGES YOU

Demi-human life does not look identical everywhere.

Nine has populations shaped by heavy industry, enormous infrastructure, and generations of working around Behemoth processing. The Hollows has its own quieter populations and traditions. Neon Gut is louder, dirtier, stranger, and full of people who have adapted to living around reality glitches and chemical runoff.

Outside SJL, communities can develop in completely different directions.
