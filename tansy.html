THE FACE OF IT

Tansy is a mature adult moth demi-human with a compact, soft masculine build and warm, worn-looking skin.

His eyes are milk-blue with black pupils and white sclera, made heavier by the drooping shape of his eyelids. His expression tends toward that particular kind of tired softness that makes somebody look like they have been standing in the same fog for three hours.

His hair is white, fuzzy, and deliberately not curly. It has the rough silhouette of Billy from Stranger Things, except fuzzier, messier, and pushed into a small back-and-shoulder mini-mullet.

There is a normal black hoop through his septum.

02

THE HOLLOWS

Tansy comes from the Hollows.

The Hollows are old residential territory that got neglected, subdivided, abandoned in pieces, reclaimed by vegetation, and generally left to become whatever the fuck they were going to become.

Heavy fog is normal here. So is the feeling that something is watching from somewhere it should not physically be able to stand.

Tansy fits here without needing the place to explain him.

AFTERIMAGE / WRECKAGE

PRETTY THINGS GET FUCKED UP TOO.

Tansy's whole visual language sits somewhere between softness, decay, and the strange beauty left behind when something has already been damaged.

He finds beauty in things that are fucked up.

That does not mean everything has to be tragic. Sometimes something is just ugly, broken, abandoned, weird, or old, and he still thinks it is worth looking at.

THE OUTLIERS

Tansy is one of the Outliers.

There is no clubhouse. There is no matching uniform. There is no official membership paperwork because that would be fucking ridiculous.

They are a wandering found family made up of people from different parts of Saint Jude's Landing and beyond.

Tansy's place in that mess is still being figured out.
