03 / CREATOR — WREN
CREATOR RECORD

Benoit is the character who answered the fucking questionnaire.

His answers are deliberately practical because that's how he experiences himself. He isn't sitting around writing a poetic character biography in his head. He's a working-class man who fixes shit, watches his people, eats a good meal, and keeps moving.

The contrast between his own description and the SJL Archivist is intentional: Benoit says “I'm just a man mostly,” while the archive is over here measuring his skeletal density and classifying his tail as a weapon.

This is also why his page should never turn him into a generic “gentle giant.” He's gentle because he chooses to be. The physical capacity for violence is very real, and the people around him know it.

05 / PHYSICAL

BUILT LIKE A WALL.

Benoit's body is enormous without being ornamental. Broad shoulders, dense muscle, heavy limbs, thick crocodilian tail, and enough mass to make ordinary furniture look temporary.

His scales cover the back, shoulders, neck, and tail. The remaining skin is rough and durable. His hands are large and calloused, with enough strength to crush bone and enough control to hold something fragile.

His face retains distinctly human proportions despite the crocodilian body: strong jaw, high cheekbones, full lips, and heavy golden eyes with vertical black pupils.

The Outliers are a loose found-family of wandering weirdos. They do illegal shit because this is Saint Jude's Landing and everybody does illegal shit. Benoit's particular contribution is keeping people alive, fixing things, stabilizing situations, and making sure the people around him have somewhere safe to sleep.
