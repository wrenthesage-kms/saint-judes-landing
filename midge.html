MOTHBALL

Midge is connected to Mothball.

Mothball is the cramped secondhand-commerce enclave inside Velvet. It is not a district.

The place is full of salvage, repair shops, old furniture, clothes, tools, books, jewelry, weird objects, stolen things, unidentified things, and approximately one million pieces of junk somebody has decided are worth keeping.

Midge belongs somewhere in that ecosystem.

MOTHBALL AT NIGHT.

The storefronts close unevenly. Some don't close at all. People move through the alleys, upper floors, stairwells, rooftops, and whatever half-legal business is still operating after midnight.

Midge can exist in that mess without the page needing to pretend Mothball is anything cleaner than it is.

THE OUTLIERS

Midge is one of the Outliers.

The Outliers are a wandering found family, not a formal organization and not a fucking criminal classification.

They come and go. They sleep wherever they can. They disappear into other districts. They show up again. Somebody is always carrying something they absolutely should not have.

Midge is part of that mess.
