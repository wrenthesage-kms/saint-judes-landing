1. PERSONAL

"Name's Riot, man. Just fucking Riot. Don't go looking for a last name, there ain't one. I'm just the guy who shows up when things get loud and leaves when the buzz wears off. Usually."

2. PHYSICAL

"I'm tall, skinny as a fuckin' rail, and I've got these goddamn dreads they're full of shit, honestly. Beads, charms, metal scraps they jingle like hell every time I move. My skin's brown, kinda warm, but I got these dark circles under my eyes like I haven't slept since the last decade. And my eyes? Dude, they're hot pink. Like, unnaturally bright. Probably fuckin' fried my retinas, but hey, they look sick in the neon, right?"

3. ANIMAL BEHAVIOR

"What's the most... uh... what's the most what? Human thing? Nah, you asked what's the most mouse thing? I'm not a mouse, bro, I'm human. But people always say I 'perch.' Like I can't just sit in a chair like a normal fucker. I gotta climb on the back of it, or sit on a windowsill, or squeeze into some goddamn crawlspace. I can't stay still. I'm always twitching, always moving. My hands are never fucking quiet."

4. PERSONALITY

"When I'm not trying to impress anyone? I'm probably fuckin' bored. That's the truth. I'm just chasing the next thing that makes the world stop feeling gray. I'm jittery, man. I'm wired. Sometimes I'm just... I'm just waiting for the next spark, you know? The next thing that makes me go 'Holy shit, look at that!'"

5. REALLY PERSONAL SHIT

"The smell of burnt wiring. Like, when you crack open a piece of fucked up tech and that sharp, metallic, ozone y shit hits your nose? Fuck yes. That's the good stuff."

6. HABITS / ROUTINE

"Wake up, probably feel like shit, find something to take the edge off something shiny, something fast then I'm out. I'm running rooftops, scavenging the Gut, talking to whatever weirdo is hanging out in the alley. I don't do 'routines,' dude. That sounds fucking boring. I just follow whatever's happening."

7. SOCIAL

"The Outliers, obviously. They're my fucking crew. Doll is a vicious little shit, but she's mine. Ammonia's... well, she's Ammonia, she's in her own world, but she's cool. And Benoit? Man, he's the anchor. Without that big bastard, we'd all be scattered to the wind. We're all fucked up, but we're fucked up together."

8. OUTLIER

"Because staying still feels like dying, man. The city tried to make me one of those drones with a job and a shitty apartment, and I was like, 'Hell nah.' Being an Outlier means I get to keep running. I stay because I found people who don't try to cage the chaos."

9. HISTORY

"Scavenging. Learning how to take things apart literally and metaphorically. Knowing what makes something work, and what makes it break. That's how I survived the Gut, and that's why I'm still here."

10. FOUND IN YOUR ROOM

"Probably a mountain of half broken electronics, some stolen trinkets I couldn't resist, empty vials of whatever I've been scraping off the streets, and probably enough tangled silver chains to choke a fuckin' demon. And don't touch my tech, bro. I'll bite your fucking hand off."
