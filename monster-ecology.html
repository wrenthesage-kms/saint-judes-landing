SAINT JUDE'S LANDING / FIELD NOTES

MONSTER
ECOLOGY

Monsters are not one fucking species. They are not all hiding in basements waiting for somebody to wander past. They live here.

Some are pests. Some are predators. Some are neighbors. Some are weird little assholes somebody keeps feeding behind the laundromat. Some have been here longer than the buildings they live in.

01 / THE BASIC PROBLEM

THE CITY HAS DIFFERENT MONSTERS BECAUSE THE CITY HAS DIFFERENT PROBLEMS.

SJL is almost two thousand years of people building shit on top of other shit, underneath other shit, through other shit, and occasionally directly inside the corpse of something that should not have existed in the first place.

So naturally, the monsters adapted.

A creature that survives in Neon Gut does not necessarily make it through Nine. Something from Velvet might be completely useless in the Hollows. Mothball has its own tiny ecosystem because apparently cursed furniture needed somewhere to reproduce.

The city changes the monsters. The monsters change the city. Nobody bothered to stop either one.

02 NEON GUT

NEON GUT MONSTERS

Everything is too loud. Everything is wet. Reality has a fucking buffering problem.

Two-faced werecreatures Slime creatures Tentacle creatures Sewer monsters Mutated rats Glitch entities Reality parasites Voice mimics Doppelgängers Living shadows Flesh-eating things Chemical-mutated wildlife Crawling things that shouldn't have legs Behemoth mutations Things that only exist during electrical failures

Neon Gut is where biology gets drunk and reality starts fucking with people for entertainment.

Chemical runoff, electrical failures, illegal modifications, old Behemoth material and whatever the fuck people dump down drains all contribute to the local ecosystem.

Some things only appear when the lights go out. Some things are perfectly physical until they suddenly are not. Some creatures mimic voices because they learned that people will open doors for somebody calling their name.

Nobody acts particularly shocked anymore.

MONSTER FUCKING: Werecreatures with two faces. Slime blobs. Tentacles. Riot claims he fucked a Behemoth carcass in Nine, so apparently the local standards have already left the building.

03 THE HOLLOWS

HOLLOWS MONSTERS

The worst part is that somebody lives there normally.

Skinwalkers Mimics Doppelgängers Face thieves Wrong-anatomy deer Black-eyed children Ghosts Revenants Shadow people Fog creatures Possessed animals Giant moths Giant spiders Things living inside walls Things underneath porches Voice imitators Things that follow you home Things that look human from far away

The Hollows does not need to turn into a haunted house because it already has houses.

Old neighborhoods, overgrown yards, fog that never quite behaves, streets that seem longer when you walk them at night. Families live here. Kids play outside. Somebody is always dragging a trash bin to the curb.

The monster problem is that sometimes the person waving from the porch is not the person who lives there.

Some creatures imitate people. Some steal faces. Some simply watch. Some follow you home and then wait until you notice.

Nobody can agree on which ones are supernatural and which ones are just animals that evolved into something deeply fucking unfortunate.

MONSTER FUCKING: Skinwalkers and other things. This is not considered an especially unusual development in the local ecosystem.

04 NINE

NINE MONSTERS

If it is huge, hungry, industrially contaminated, or growing out of a corpse, somebody in Nine has probably seen it before.

Behemoths Lesser Behemoths Juvenile Behemoths Mutated Behemoths Behemoth hybrids Chemical-runoff amphibians Giant frogs Giant toads Mutated salamanders Toxic aquatic creatures Bone creatures Living bone masses Carcass-feeders Creatures growing inside carcasses Giant beetles Giant roaches Giant centipedes Mantis monsters Industrial mutations Parasites Symbiotic organisms Things accidentally created by industrial experimentation

Nine is not a monster factory.

It is a functioning industrial district where people work, eat, sleep, complain about their shifts, repair machinery, raise families, and occasionally have to move because something enormous has decided to nest inside the processing plant.

Behemoth remains are everywhere. Bone becomes infrastructure. Blood becomes fuel and chemical feedstock. Flesh becomes an ecosystem.

A dead Behemoth does not necessarily stay dead in any useful sense. Things eat it. Things hatch inside it. Things grow through it. Things evolve around it.

Sometimes industrial experimentation creates something new. Sometimes nobody admits they were experimenting.

MONSTER FUCKING: Whatever monsters happen to be there. Riot fucking a Behemoth carcass is still a thing people know happened. Nobody in Nine needs the story explained twice.

05 VELVET

VELVET MONSTERS

Some families have money. Some have curses. Some have both.

Vampires Witches Revenants Ghosts Cursed people Possessed people Doppelgängers Shapeshifters Skinwalkers Mimics Haunted objects Cursed heirlooms Living dolls Living mannequins Moving portraits Wrong mirrors House-bound entities Parasites Fungal infestations Behemoth-tainted bloodlines Inherited mutations Beautiful monsters Old-family horrors Things summoned into houses Things that have been "in the family" for generations

Velvet has monsters with paperwork.

Not necessarily legal paperwork. More like family records, inheritance documents, old portraits, bloodline charts, locked rooms, sealed basements, and somebody's grandmother insisting that the thing in the attic has always been there.

The local ecology is tied to inheritance.

Curses get passed down. Mutations get passed down. Houses remember their occupants. Objects become haunted because somebody kept them long enough for something to happen.

Some monsters are beautiful. Some are elegant. Some look completely human. Some are absolutely fucking not.

MONSTER FUCKING: Old bloodlines are old bloodlines. Velvet has had several thousand years to make the family tree everybody else's problem.

06 MOTHBALL / VELVET

MOTHBALL MONSTERS

Somebody put it on a shelf and sold it for twelve dollars.

Haunted dolls Living dolls Possessed toys Toy-sized creatures Mimics Little face thieves Creepy household spirits Feral cats Giant mice Rats Moths Spiders Parasites Things hiding in thrifted furniture Things living inside old mattresses Cursed clothing Haunted jewelry Objects that move when nobody is looking Things accidentally brought home from the thrift market Tiny monsters nesting inside old buildings Whatever weird shit people accidentally sell

Mothball is inside Velvet.

It is not its own district. It is an enclave built around commerce: thrift stores, antique shops, collector boutiques, repair shops, salvage dealers, family businesses and people who somehow know which basement has the good shit.

The monsters here tend to be small, domestic, object-bound, or extremely good at hiding inside things people already own.

The ecosystem is basically: somebody found something weird, somebody bought it, somebody brought it home, and now there are six of them living in the mattress.

MONSTER FUCKING: Probably happening. Mothball is not exactly known for enforcing boundaries between "living thing" and "thing somebody found in a box."
DOLL'S TERRITORY
07 PUGAD / WORLD OUTSIDE

PUGAD MONSTERS

The ecosystem is allowed to be an ecosystem here.

Ordinary local wildlife Oversized wildlife Strange Behemoth-descended animals Farm animals Forest creatures River creatures Wetland creatures Birds Snakes Frogs Insects Beneficial symbiotic organisms Demi-humans living as part of the population

Pugad is outside SJL.

It is rural. Green. Practical. Generational. People farm, run small businesses, trade with one another, raise families and know which parts of the woods you do not fuck around in.

The local monsters are closer to wildlife than urban horror. Some are strange because Behemoth biology has been leaking into ecosystems for generations.

Some are huge. Some are dangerous. Some are useful. Some are just animals.

Demi-humans are not monsters here. They are people.

And yes, monsters fuck. That is how Cheche's family exists. That is how demi-humans exist in the first place. Nobody needs a fucking lecture about it.

MONSTER FUCKING: Woodland and wild-animal shit. Instinctive, biological, sometimes weird as hell. This is part of the ecosystem, not a supernatural event.
CHECHE'S HOME
08 / THE IMPORTANT PART

THE MONSTERS ARE PART OF THE CITY.

People know where the dangerous ones live. They know which ones are edible. They know which ones you can keep as pets. They know which ones will eat your dog. They know which ones will imitate your dead mother. They know which ones you absolutely should not fuck.

Mostly.

There is no single monster hierarchy. There is no master taxonomy. There is no government-issued book called So You Found A Monster: A Citizen's Guide.

There are just people who have lived here long enough to know what lives underneath their fucking porch.

SAINT JUDE'S LANDING THE CITY IS ALIVE. UNFORTUNATELY.
