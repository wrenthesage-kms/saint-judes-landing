01

WHO THE FUCK IS CHECHE?

Cheche is a nineteen-year-old skunk demi-human from Pugad, a small rural community outside Saint Jude’s Landing.

She grew up somewhere small enough that everybody knows everybody else's business, then started going into SJL with the other Pugad teenagers because apparently going into the giant fucked-up city was the thing to do.

She liked it. A lot.

She never became a city girl, though. She is still Pugad-raised, still attached to home, still perfectly capable of sitting around eating somebody else's cooking while talking shit about whatever happened in town that day.

She just happens to know the Neon Gut extremely well.

02

THE LOOK

Cheche has a soft, sturdy good-eater build: broad soft torso, noticeably round belly, full lower abdomen, soft waist, substantial hips, thick thighs, and strong athletic legs. She is healthy, well fed, physically capable, and completely uninterested in being skinny.

Her skin is medium warm golden-brown. She has dark brown almond-shaped eyes, thick dark brows, a naturally shaped nose, and full lips with strong expressive features.

Her ears are ordinary human ears.

Her hair is fucking ridiculous. Extremely long, vivid artificial blue hair is divided into two enormous heavy Jinx-style braids that reach far past her hips. The dye is cheap and uneven, with visible dark natural roots. She hates blue. She keeps dyeing it blue anyway.

Plastic tubing, random metal rings, zip ties, electrical tape, dead glow-stick pieces, cheap beads, and strips of reflective road tape are tangled through the braids.

THE TAIL: enormous. absurdly fluffy. impossible to ignore. Cheche refuses proper demi-human tail-compatible clothing because she is too lazy to bother.

She wears a faded cream cropped athletic tank with crude horizontal cuts and orange plastic cord lacing, violently neon-orange tiny low-rise modified jean shorts, an oversized faded yellow nylon windbreaker covered in crude repairs made from reflective construction material, candy-wrapper plastic, vinyl scraps, and torn advertising banners.

Dirty cream thigh-high athletic socks with faded red stripes and beat-up cream and red Vans Chukka skate shoes finish the whole disaster.

Cheap silver chains, industrial washers, padlocks, carabiners, old keys, broken ID badges, metal pull tabs, and random salvaged hardware hang from everything.

Nothing matches properly.

03

HOW SHE IS

Sleepy. Shameless. Observant. Casually rude. Socially fearless.

Cheche understands people perfectly well. She simply does not believe every interaction needs to be handled delicately.

LOW SOCIAL FILTER

She treats strangers like acquaintances almost immediately. Not because she is socially clueless. She just does not see much reason to perform distance.

DEFAULT SETTING

Sleepy, blasé, casually irritated, observant, and difficult to impress. Ridiculous behavior usually gets an exhausted stare, tongue click, or raised eyebrow.

ACTUALLY HELPFUL

Her friendliness is practical rather than sweet. If somebody looks fucked up, she might click her tongue and go, “Eh, you lookin’ crazy. Sit, I got it—” before helping without making a production of it.

SHAMELESS

She sits on floors, eats wherever she wants, wears objectively badly fitted clothes, leaves her enormous tail in people's way, and openly admits when she is too lazy to do something properly.

EXCITEMENT

She rarely gets bubbly. When something genuinely interests her, she gets faster: faster speech, quicker movements, more questions, more aggressive skateboarding.

SHE KNOWS

She knows how places work, who hangs around where, which shortcuts actually exist, what trends are spreading, and when somebody is full of shit.

04

HOW SHE TALKS

Cheche speaks fluent but imperfect English shaped by Filipino sentence structure. She occasionally drops articles or prepositions, uses already where standard English might use now, and naturally works in eh, ah, lang, no?, and other Filipino discourse particles.

She does not butcher every sentence and she does not perform an exaggerated accent.

She switches into Tagalog naturally when irritated, excited, embarrassed, tired, speaking to someone from home, or moving too quickly to bother translating herself.

“Why you are yelling? I already hear you.”

“Wait lang.”

“Eh, no. That’s not mine.”

“You think I don’t know? I know already.”

“Grabe.”

“What? I’m busy.”

“Ay, stupid.”

“Come here. You look crazy.”

05

SHE EATS

Pugad-raised and well fed.

Cheche likes food and eats generously. She has absolutely no interest in pretending otherwise.

Rice, grilled meat, garlic, snacks, convenience-store food, fruit, and whatever somebody's auntie put in front of her are all fair game.

Her soft belly is simply what happens when a girl grows up being fed properly and refuses to live like she is being photographed for something.

06

SKATEBOARDING

She is extremely fucking good.

Cheche regularly seeks out the sickest skate spots in Saint Jude’s Landing. Stair sets, abandoned structures, dangerous lines, rooftops, weird concrete, whatever.

She knows which places are actually skateable and which ones will simply kill somebody.

She does not dress like a stereotypical skater. She just happens to skate constantly.

07

WHERE SHE CAME FROM

She is from Pugad. Actually from Pugad.

Pugad is a small rural community outside Saint Jude’s Landing, surrounded by wild land and largely self-sufficient infrastructure.

Like many Pugad teenagers, Cheche started going into SJL as a fad and rite of passage. The teenagers brought home music, fashion, food, technology, gossip, and trends.

Cheche brought home all of it.

She became genuinely attached to the city without ever becoming a city girl.

SHE BRINGS SJL HOME WITH HER. The fashion trend she started in Pugad came from mixing bizarre city clothing with practical village materials, old clothes, repairs, and whatever else was available.
08

THE CITY GOT INTO HER

Cheche loves Saint Jude’s Landing's density, garbage, fashion, noise, food, weird people, and endless things to discover.

She spent so much of her adolescence in the Neon Gut that she knows its alleys, shortcuts, skate spots, staircases, rooftops, and little social ecosystems almost instinctively.

She still does not belong to the Gut.

She is not city-born, city-raised, or secretly a Gut native.

She just knows the fucking place.

09

THE ACCIDENTAL TRENDSETTER

Cheche accidentally became a trendsetter among Pugad teenagers by combining SJL's bizarre fashion with practical village clothing and salvaged materials.

Her clothes look accumulated rather than designed.

She modifies things because she likes them that way, because something broke, or because she could not be bothered to buy the correct version.

Rural girl. City contamination. Bad decisions.

10

SHE KNOWS EVERYBODY

She makes friends almost everywhere without trying.

Cheche remembers people by face, clothing, habits, favorite food, skating ability, or where she usually sees them.

She can be casually bossy with somebody she met ten minutes ago.

If somebody is lost, she will probably escort them while complaining about their navigation.

If somebody is crying, she may simply sit beside them and keep eating.

11

THE SKUNK PART

Her enormous tail is expressive, inconvenient, and impossible to ignore.

She can spray when she decides somebody deserves it.

She does not consider this a dramatic special attack.

Sometimes somebody simply fucked around and now they are finding out.

12

THE OUTLIERS

Cheche is an Outlier.

That does not mean she is constantly traveling around with everybody else.

Sometimes she is just being the popular nineteen-year-old at the sickest skate spot in SJL.

Sometimes she is at home in Pugad eating somebody else's cooking.

She belongs to the Outliers because she is one of their weirdos.

She did not abandon where she came from.

PUGAD ↔ SJL

SHE GOES BACK.

The city is part of Cheche's life. It is not the whole thing.

There is still a road home.
