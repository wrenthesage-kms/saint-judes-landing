SAINT JUDE'S LANDING

An ancient city that was never actually planned. Roughly nineteen hundred years of construction, trade, migration, industry, disasters, bad decisions, abandoned buildings, strange businesses, dead things, living things, and people who simply refused to leave.

ARCHIVE NOTE DO NOT READ THIS LIKE A TOURIST BROCHURE

Saint Jude's Landing is not a clean historical site. It is not a city that was built once and maintained afterward.

It accumulated.

People built things. Other people built over them. Somebody moved in. Somebody else opened a business. Somebody dug a tunnel. Somebody flooded it. Somebody built a road over the tunnel. Somebody built an apartment over the road.

Then nineteen hundred years happened.

CITY / README

THE CITY

What Saint Jude's Landing actually is, without pretending anybody fully understands it.

ARCHITECTURAL SURVEY NON-LINEAR geometry disputed
ORIGIN / FILE 0001

A DOCK, BASICALLY.

The first settlement was small enough that calling it a city would have been ridiculous. There was a wooden dock, storage space, several houses, a rough inland road, and freshwater nearby. People built where the ground would support them because there was no reason to do anything else.

No master plan was waiting for them. Nobody drew out elegant avenues and designated future districts. People put buildings where they needed buildings. They dug drainage after drainage became a problem. They widened roads when carts could no longer get through them. Then somebody built on top of that.

TRADE

EVERYTHING PASSED THROUGH.

Fish, salt, grain, leather, metal, tools, manufactured goods, stolen goods, smuggled goods, illegal goods, things that technically belonged to someone else, and things nobody wanted to admit existed.

THE LANDING

SO WHO THE FUCK WAS JUDE?

Nobody knows.

The oldest surviving references call him “Jude of the Landing.” One account describes a fisherman. Another describes a soldier. One particularly questionable source insists Jude was a drunk who stole a boat and accidentally founded the settlement.

There is no reliable evidence that he was ever a saint.

At some point somebody started calling him Saint Jude. Nobody corrected them. The name survived.

MODERN SAINT JUDE

TECHNICALLY, THERE IS A GOVERNMENT.

There is a mayor. There are courts. There are police. There are taxes, permits, departments, zoning regulations, municipal offices, and enough paperwork to prove that somebody is theoretically in charge.

Actual authority is considerably messier.

Corporations, factories, unions, families, gangs, landlords, private security, smugglers, religious organizations, wealthy individuals, businesses, and heavily armed groups all control pieces of the city in practice.

Some streets answer to nobody. Some buildings have their own rules. Some businesses do not accept official currency. Some residents have not paid municipal taxes in decades.

Somehow, the city continues functioning.

THE FIRST BEHEMOTH

An enormous carcass washes ashore.

It is larger than the settlement. Its bones are unusually dense. Its hide resists fire. Its blood contaminates the surrounding land for years.

People harvest everything they can remove.

CITY / TERRITORY

DISTRICTS

Five major identities. Not five clean shapes on a map.

IMPORTANT: District boundaries are social, physical, historical, and sometimes vertical. A building can connect to another district without technically being in it. Mothball is an enclave inside Velvet, not a sixth district.
01 / NEON GUT

THE NEON GUT

ORIGIN: AMMONIA / RIOT

The vertical heartbeat of the city's underbelly. The Gut grew around commerce, entertainment, smuggling, cheap housing, street economies, illegal businesses, and every other industry that benefits from not asking too many questions.

Flickering magenta and cyan signs hang over black rainwater. Burnt electronics mix with street food. Sirens compete with bass from illegal clubs. People shout because nobody can hear otherwise.

SMUGGLERS CLUBS STREET ECONOMY ADRENALINE JUNKIES
02 / LIMINAL

THE HOLLOWS

Old residential territory that was gradually abandoned, neglected, subdivided, forgotten, and reclaimed.

Fog sits heavy here. Wind moves through broken windows. Yards disappear under vegetation that looks almost normal until somebody gets close.

People come here because they want to be left alone. Some are hiding. Some simply stopped wanting to participate in the rest of the city.

FOG ABANDONED HOMES OVERGROWTH PRIVACY
03 / INDUSTRIAL

NINE

Industrial mass. Factories, processing plants, warehouses, transport systems, foundries, chemical facilities, worker housing, and machinery large enough to make people look temporary.

Behemoth remains are not unusual here. Some were processed. Some were incorporated into machines. Some were too large to move.

In parts of Nine, the distinction between building, machine, and carcass has stopped being useful.

FACTORIES OIL HOT IRON BEHEMOTH REMAINS
04 / OLD MONEY

VELVET

Old wealth that never quite managed to disappear. Mansions, institutions, estates, old businesses, inherited properties, and buildings that were once expensive enough to justify maintaining them.

Now there is mold on the walls, mildew in the curtains, damp stone under expensive carpets, and spiderwebs hanging from chandeliers nobody can afford to replace.

It is beautiful sometimes. It is grotesque sometimes. Usually it is both.

OLD MONEY MANSIONS DECAY MOTHBALL
04.5 / ENCLAVE / MARKET

MOTHBALL

Mothball is physically part of Velvet but functions like its own little ecosystem.

Narrow storefronts, cramped alleys, upper floors packed with inventory, basements used as storage, old buildings subdivided into whatever businesses fit inside them.

Furniture, jewelry, clothes, tools, books, weapons, religious objects, broken machines, family heirlooms, stolen goods, industrial components, behemoth-derived materials, and unidentified objects move through here.

PEOPLE / UNOFFICIAL RECORD

THE OUTLIERS

Four people who found each other and became difficult to separate.

There is no formal hierarchy. There is no grand mission. There is no sensible reason they should work as well as they do.

“You got somewhere to sleep?”
“No.”
“Come on.”

That is enough to start a family in Saint Jude's Landing.

MISC / UNSORTED

FIELD NOTES

Things worth knowing before you start walking around.

NOTE 001

THE MAP IS WRONG.

Every map is wrong. Some are simply less wrong than others.

NOTE 002

MOTHBALL IS NOT A DISTRICT.

It is an enclave and market inside Velvet. Calling it a sixth district will get you corrected by somebody who has probably been stabbed over this.

NOTE 003

THE CITY IS NOT A SETTING.

It is the accumulated result of nineteen hundred years of people making decisions and then building more shit on top of those decisions.

NOTE 004

NOT EVERYTHING IS EXPLAINED.

Some records are missing. Some structures are unidentified. Some things simply have no surviving explanation.

NOTE 005

PEOPLE ARE THE CITY.

The Landing is full of families, crews, gangs, workers, artists, criminals, collectors, fugitives, scientists, addicts, businesses, religious groups, weird little communities, and people who just found somewhere to sleep.

NOTE 006

IF YOU HEAR SOMETHING MOVING BELOW YOU—

Do not assume it is a pipe.
