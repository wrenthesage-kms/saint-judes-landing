WORLDSET / PRIMARY RECORD

SAINT JUDE'S
LANDING

Ancient. Crowded. Industrial. Layered. Difficult to control. Still inhabited. Still changing. Still accumulating shit.

01 DO NOT READ THIS LIKE A TOURIST BROCHURE.

SJL is not a curated attraction. It is an approximately 1,900-year-old city that kept building itself because nobody ever successfully convinced it to stop.

Saint Jude's Landing began as a wooden dock, storage structures, several houses, a rough inland road, and access to freshwater. People built where the ground seemed useful. Then somebody built beside them. Then above them. Then below them.

Trade expanded. Buildings changed hands. Roads were buried. Foundations became basements. Basements became tunnels. Infrastructure was built over infrastructure. Businesses moved into abandoned buildings. Somebody eventually put another building on top of that.

NOBODY DESIGNED THIS PLACE.

It accumulated.

THE CITY IS NOT A SETTING. IT IS A CITY.

People work here. They raise children here. They run stores, repair buildings, fall in love, get divorced, make art, go to school, get drunk, start businesses, lose jobs, commit crimes, gossip, go home, wake up, and have boring Tuesdays.

Horror exists inside ordinary life rather than replacing it. The city is not constantly screaming. Most of the time someone is just trying to get through their shift.

SJL functions despite everything because people keep making it function.

AGE ~1,900 YEARS

The city has been accumulating since the original Landing.

GROUND LEVEL UNRELIABLE

There is no single ground floor anymore.

AUTHORITY FRAGMENTED

Government exists. Control is another question.

ARCHIVAL RECORD / CHRONOLOGY

HISTORY

Everything has a reason. Most of those reasons have been forgotten.

APPROX. 1,900 YEARS OF ACCUMULATION
~1900 YEARS AGO

THE LANDING

The earliest settlement begins around a wooden dock, storage structures, several houses, a rough inland road, and freshwater. The location is useful. People stay.

EARLY CENTURIES

TRADE TAKES OVER

Fish, salt, grain, leather, metal, tools, manufactured goods, stolen goods, smuggled goods, illegal goods, things that technically belonged to someone else, and things nobody wanted to admit existed begin passing through the Landing.

The settlement grows because trade keeps giving people reasons to remain.

~1100 YEARS AGO

THE FIRST BEHEMOTH

The first recorded Behemoth carcass washes ashore.

It is larger than the settlement itself. Its bones are unusually dense. Its hide resists fire. Its blood contaminates the surrounding land for years.

People harvest everything.

At first the material is useful. Then it becomes valuable. Eventually it becomes profitable enough to change the entire economy of the city.

FOLLOWING CENTURIES

INDUSTRY

Behemoth-derived materials become construction materials, tools, fuel, chemicals, manufactured goods, recreational substances, and commercial products.

The city grows around the industry because the industry is already growing around the city.

BIOLOGICAL / INDUSTRIAL RECORD

BEHEMOTHS

Nobody agrees what they are.

Some resemble animals. Some do not. Some decay normally. Some do not. Some are found dead. Some are found alive. Some keep moving after death.

THE ECONOMY

Bone, teeth, hide, blood, organs, claws, glands, and other tissue became useful long before anyone fully understood what they were.

THE PROBLEM

SJL built an entire industrial economy around biologically inconsistent creatures whose behavior does not reliably obey ordinary expectations.

THE RESULT

Eventually, nobody could cleanly separate the city from the creatures that helped build it.

SOCIAL IDENTITY / CURRENT ROSTER

THE OUTLIERS

A loose found-family of wandering weirdos from different corners of Saint Jude's Landing and beyond.

The Outliers started with Ammonia, a legendary graffiti artist who travels throughout Saint Jude's Landing leaving and creating murals. Along the way, she finds other outlier weirdos who decide to join the traveling and stay as a loose family.

Not every Outlier is always present. Some live at home. Some are wandering elsewhere. Some are spending time in another district. Some disappear for a while.

The Outliers do not have a permanent home. They have bunkers, tunnels, abandoned rooms, inns, apartments, rooftops, hideouts, and other places scattered throughout every district.

OUTLIER IS A SOCIAL IDENTITY. NOT A CRIMINAL CLASSIFICATION.
