01 DON'T EXPECT THE CITY TO MAKE SENSE.

It isn't supposed to.

Saint Jude's Landing has been here for roughly 1,900 years. Nobody had the foresight to stop building on top of everything that came before them, so now there are streets under streets, buildings inside buildings, tunnels underneath things that probably shouldn't have tunnels underneath them, and entire neighborhoods that have been rebuilt so many times nobody can agree where the original version was.

It started small.

A wooden dock. Storage. A few houses. A rough road inland. Freshwater.

People came because the place was useful.

Then more people came. Somebody built beside the houses. Somebody else built above that. Somebody dug underneath it. Roads got buried. Foundations became basements. Basements became tunnels. Old buildings got turned into new buildings without anybody bothering to remove the old building first.

IT JUST KEPT ACCUMULATING.

That's Saint Jude's Landing.

PEOPLE ACTUALLY LIVE HERE.

This is the part people forget when they start describing SJL like it's some giant haunted industrial set piece.

People have apartments here. People have houses here. People have shitty little corner stores and restaurants and offices and schools and repair shops and laundromats and bars and workplaces and places they absolutely should not be renting but somehow have been renting for twelve years.

Families live here. Kids grow up here. People get married. People get divorced. People make art. People get fired. People start businesses. People go to work half asleep. Somebody is always trying to fix something.

Most people are not waiting for a supernatural event to happen. Most people are trying to get through fucking Tuesday.

The weird shit exists inside ordinary life. It doesn't replace it.

THE CITY WORKS BECAUSE PEOPLE KEEP MAKING IT WORK.

~1900 YEARS AGO

THE LANDING

Wooden dock. Storage. Several houses. A rough road inland. Freshwater.

That's basically it.

It was useful, so people stayed.

EARLY CENTURIES

PEOPLE STARTED TRADING SHIT

Fish. Salt. Grain. Leather. Metal. Tools. Manufactured goods. Stolen goods. Smuggled goods. Things nobody wanted to explain at customs.

The settlement got bigger because trade gave people reasons to keep coming back.

~1100 YEARS AGO

THE FIRST BEHEMOTH

A Behemoth carcass washes ashore.

It is enormous. Bigger than anything the settlement has ever seen.

The bones are dense. The hide is useful. The blood does things to the surrounding environment that nobody really understands.

PEOPLE HARVEST EVERYTHING.

At first, because they can use it.

Then because it's valuable.

Eventually people figure out how much of civilization can be built out of something that should not exist in the first place.

FOLLOWING CENTURIES

WELL, NOW WE HAVE AN INDUSTRY

Behemoth bone becomes material. Hide becomes material. Blood becomes useful. Teeth become useful. Glands become useful. Organs become useful. Eventually somebody figures out a use for basically everything.

Construction grows around the industry. The industry grows around the city.

After enough time, nobody remembers where one ends and the other begins.

THE LAST FEW CENTURIES

EVERYTHING KEEPS GETTING WEIRDER

Industry gets bigger. The city gets denser. People keep experimenting with things they probably shouldn't. Monsters become part of the environment instead of some distant bedtime story.

And somehow everybody just keeps going to work.

CURRENT

THIS IS THE VERSION WE'RE STUCK WITH

Saint Jude's Landing is old enough that nobody has a complete record of what happened here.

Parts of the city have been demolished and rebuilt. Parts have been buried. Parts have been forgotten. Parts have been built around.

New shit keeps appearing anyway.

Saint Jude's Landing was built in layers because apparently nobody ever looked at an existing street and thought, “maybe we should leave this one alone.”

Streets connect to tunnels. Shops have rooms above and below them. Rooftops become roads. Old foundations become somebody else's basement.

Local knowledge is worth more than a map.

THE BIG FUCKING ONES

BEHEMOTHS

Behemoths are the reason half this city is made out of things that look like they should still have organs in them.

They are enormous, biologically inconsistent creatures. Some resemble animals. Some barely resemble anything recognizable. Their bodies don't always behave the way bodies are supposed to.

People learned how to use them.

PEOPLE GOT REALLY FUCKING GOOD AT IT.

WHAT GETS TAKEN

Bone. Teeth. Hide. Blood. Organs. Claws. Glands. Whatever else somebody has figured out how to harvest.

WHAT IT BECOMES

Construction materials. Tools. Fuel. Chemicals. Manufactured goods. Recreational products. Things you have probably touched without knowing what they came from.

THE WEIRD PART

THE OTHER SHIT

MONSTERS

Behemoths are not monsters. Monsters are not demi-humans.

Those categories get messy as hell, but they are not the same thing.

SJL has monsters because SJL has been here for a very, very long time. Some are biological. Some aren't. Some are predators. Some are parasites. Some imitate people. Some barely qualify as living things.

Some are dangerous. Some are pests. Some are fucking weird.

SOME OF THEM ARE JUST GUYS.

THE WEIRDOS

THE OUTLIERS

People who kept finding each other.

The Outliers started with Ammonia.

She's a legendary graffiti artist who moves all over Saint Jude's Landing, painting wherever she feels like painting and collecting weird people along the way.

Eventually those people became a loose family.

They don't live together. They don't have a headquarters. They don't have matching jackets or a constitution or whatever the fuck a formal organization is supposed to have.

They have apartments. Bunkers. Rooftops. Tunnels. Abandoned rooms. Inns. Hideouts. Other people's couches.

Sometimes they disappear for a while. Sometimes somebody shows up unexpectedly. Sometimes they get into illegal shit together.

That's not especially unusual in SJL.

OUTLIER IS A SOCIAL IDENTITY.

It isn't a criminal classification. It isn't a gang. It isn't a business. Nobody has membership cards.
