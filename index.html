CITY RECORD // 001

There is no single ground level.

Saint Jude's Landing is approximately 1,900 years old. Nobody designed it. It accumulated.

A wooden dock became storage. Storage became houses. Houses became streets. Streets became neighborhoods. Neighborhoods became factories, markets, tunnels, basements, bridges, rooftops, utility corridors, abandoned buildings, businesses, illegal businesses, and buildings built directly on top of things that were already there.

Then people kept building.

There is no master plan. There is no clean boundary between old and new. There is no point at which the city stopped becoming itself.

DO NOT ASSUME

The map is wrong. The city is not.

SJL

SJL functions despite everything because people keep making it function.

STRUCTURAL RECORD

Built through accumulation.

The city grew around whatever was already there. Old roads remain underneath newer roads. Buildings have basements underneath older foundations. Utility systems pass through abandoned structures. New construction occasionally reveals rooms nobody remembers building.

Some routes exist because somebody needed to get somewhere three hundred years ago. Some exist because somebody knocked a hole through a wall. Some exist because the wall was never actually where the records said it was.

PUBLIC RECORD

Everything passes through.

Fish. Salt. Grain. Leather. Metal. Tools. Manufactured goods. Stolen goods. Smuggled goods. Illegal goods. Things that technically belonged to someone else.

Things nobody wanted to admit existed.

CIVIC CONDITION

Technically, there is a government.

Mayor. Courts. Police. Taxes. Permits. Zoning. Departments. Municipal offices.

Actual authority is fragmented between corporations, factories, unions, families, gangs, landlords, private security, smugglers, religious groups, wealthy people, and businesses.

HISTORICAL RECORD // 002

Everything else happened afterward.

Dates are approximate. Records disagree. Some records are lying.

~1,900 YEARS AGO

THE LANDING

The earliest settlement begins as a wooden dock, storage, several houses, a rough road inland, and access to freshwater.

People build wherever the ground supports them. There is no master plan.

EARLY CENTURIES

TRADE TAKES OVER

The settlement becomes a useful place to move goods, people, materials, and information.

The dock becomes a city because people keep finding reasons to stay.

~1,100 YEARS AGO
MAJOR EVENT

THE FIRST BEHEMOTH

A carcass washes ashore larger than the settlement itself.

Its bones are unusually dense. Its hide resists fire. Its blood contaminates the surrounding land for years.

People harvest everything they can.

Behemoth material becomes useful. Then valuable. Then profitable.

Eventually an entire industrial economy grows around it.

FOLLOWING CENTURIES

INDUSTRY

Processing facilities, factories, chemical plants, transport systems, worker housing, warehouses, foundries, and associated infrastructure spread through the city.

Behemoth remains become construction material, fuel, chemicals, tools, manufactured goods, recreational substances, and commercial products.

CURRENT RECORD

OUTSIDE GOVERNMENTS STOP TRYING.

At some point, outside governments decide that Saint Jude's Landing is too expensive, too complicated, too entrenched, and still too useful to dismantle.

The practical solution becomes leaving it alone.

No one is coming to fix it.

ORIGIN FIGURE

JUDE OF THE LANDING

The oldest surviving references call him “Jude of the Landing.”

One account describes a fisherman. Another describes a soldier. One particularly questionable source insists Jude was a drunk who stole a boat and accidentally founded the settlement.

There is no reliable evidence that he was ever a saint. At some point somebody started calling him Saint Jude. Nobody corrected them.

The name survived.

BIOLOGICAL / INDUSTRIAL RECORD // 004

BEHEMOTHS

Nobody agrees what they are. Everyone agrees they are useful.

PRIMARY INDUSTRIAL DRIVER

Biologically inconsistent. Physically inconvenient. Extremely profitable.

Some resemble animals. Some do not. Some decay normally. Some do not. Some are found dead. Some are found alive. Some continue moving after death.

The city has spent approximately eleven centuries learning what can be removed from them and what can be done with it.

Bone. Teeth. Hide. Blood. Organs. Claws. Glands. Other tissue.

Once harvested, those materials enter ordinary life. Buildings contain them. Machines contain them. Chemicals contain them. People buy products made from them without necessarily knowing what they are.

IMPORTANT

“Behemoth” is a practical word, not a complete biological classification.

Do not assume two specimens belong to the same species because they share a shape.

Do not assume something is dead because it is no longer breathing.

SOCIAL RECORD // 006

THE OUTLIERS

Not an organization. Not a gang. Not a clubhouse.

The Outliers are a loose found-family of wandering weirdos from different corners of Saint Jude's Landing and beyond.

The Outliers started with Ammonia, a legendary graffiti artist who travels throughout Saint Jude's Landing leaving and creating murals. Along the way, she finds other outlier weirdos who decide to join the traveling and stay as a loose family.

Not every Outlier is always present. Some live at home. Some are wandering elsewhere. Some are spending time in another district. Some disappear for a while.

They do not have a permanent home. They have bunkers, tunnels, abandoned rooms, inns, apartments, rooftops, hideouts, and other places scattered throughout every district.

DEFINITION OUTLIER = SOCIAL IDENTITY

They do illegal shit. So does literally every other fucking person in Saint Jude's Landing.

Illegal activity is normal in SJL and is not inherently what defines the Outliers.
