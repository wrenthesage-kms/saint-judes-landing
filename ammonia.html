OTHER BAT SHIT

She's roughly 152 cm / 5'0". Her skin has little to no standard human body hair, aside from fine nearly invisible down along the spine. It's sleek and leathery, with slightly pebbled high-friction areas around the knees and elbows. Her body temperature runs several degrees below a human baseline.

Her ears articulate independently and can rotate through a huge range of motion. She picks up mechanical vibration, distant electrical activity, and movement other people don't notice. Echolocation is continuous and unconscious, becoming more pronounced when she's climbing or moving through darkness.

Her low-light vision, movement detection, and contrast perception are considerably better than a human's. She has no wings, flight membranes, or skeletal structures associated with flight. The bat classification comes from the cranial, dermal, ocular, and auditory characteristics.

ART

MAKE THE GREY GO AWAY.

“I don't plan. Planning is for people who fear mistakes.”

Her work is improvisational. Cracks, rust, water damage, old paint, exposed infrastructure, existing graffiti, weird stains—none of it gets treated as a blank surface. It becomes part of the composition.

Her recurring imagery includes Behemoths, impossible anatomy, creatures inside infrastructure, industrial decay, and things that absolutely should not exist.

The signature is three parallel tapering slashes, like a claw drag. Sometimes her name appears beside it. The slashes are the real signature.
