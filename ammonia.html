THE WALL TELLS HER WHAT IT WANTS TO BE

“I don't plan. Planning is for people who fear mistakes.”

Subject's graffiti practice is primarily improvisational. She incorporates cracks, rust, water damage, old paint, structural texture, and existing graffiti into compositions rather than treating the wall as a blank surface.

Her recurring imagery includes Behemoths, impossible anatomy, creatures inhabiting infrastructure, industrial decay, and entities described by witnesses as “things that shouldn't exist.”

Signature mark consists of three parallel tapering slashes resembling a claw drag. The subject occasionally produces larger versions of her name, but does not consider the name itself the primary signature.
