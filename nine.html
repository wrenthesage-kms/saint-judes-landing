Nine is where Saint Jude's Landing turns fucking enormous.

Factories, warehouses, foundries, chemical facilities, processing plants, transport systems, worker housing, machine yards, loading platforms, service corridors, and whatever else somebody decided could reasonably be built here.

Some buildings have been expanded so many times that nobody remembers what the original structure was. Some machinery is older than the businesses operating around it. Some things have been incorporated directly into the infrastructure because removing them would've been more expensive than simply building around them.

That is basically Nine's entire philosophy.

01

THE
WORK

Somebody has to make all this shit useful.

Behemoth remains aren't valuable because they are pretty. They're valuable because Saint Jude's Landing figured out approximately fourteen thousand ways to turn something impossible into a raw material.

Bone becomes structural material. Hide becomes industrial material. Teeth become tools, ornaments, components, weapons, whatever somebody can sell. Blood and organs become chemicals, fuel, medicines, recreational substances, and things nobody is particularly interested in putting on a label.

02

PEOPLE
LIVE HERE

Nine has families, landlords, corner stores, shitty apartments, schools, bars, restaurants, arguments, laundry, birthdays and people who have absolutely no interest in hearing about the latest fucking Behemoth incident.

Industrial work is everywhere, but it isn't the entirety of life here.

People get off shift and go home. They complain about rent. Somebody's kid is always riding a bike where they absolutely should not be riding it. Someone leaves their laundry in the communal machine for six hours. Someone else is selling cigarettes outside. The factory sirens become background noise.

AFTER DARK

NIGHT
SHIFT

Nine doesn't shut down at night.

The lights change. The shift changes. Trucks keep moving. Processing floors keep running. Somebody is welding something in a building that probably should have been condemned thirty years ago.

The whole district has a different rhythm after dark, but it never becomes quiet.

03

THE
YARD

Some of these things are too fucking big to move.

Behemoths are processed wherever they can be processed. Sometimes that means moving them through the city. Sometimes it means cutting them apart where they landed. Sometimes the answer is simply: build around it.

There are carcasses in Nine that have become landmarks without anybody formally deciding they were landmarks.

THING PEOPLE HAVE TO REMEMBER Some Behemoths continue moving after death.

NINE SPECIALTY

MACHINE
/ CARCASS

There are machines in Nine that are no longer entirely machines.

Metal gets fused with flesh. Old processing equipment grows things. Organic material becomes load-bearing. Pipes develop teeth.

Eventually somebody puts a warning sign on it, and eventually somebody ignores the warning sign.

BELOW

Nine goes down.

Old foundations, drainage systems, buried roads, utility tunnels, forgotten rooms, industrial infrastructure and whatever was already underneath the thing somebody decided to build.

There is no clean boundary between the current Nine and the Nine underneath it.
