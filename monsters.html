01 / BASIC PROBLEM

THE CITY HAS A LOT OF THINGS IN IT.

Some of them are people. Some of them are Behemoths. Some of them are animals. Some of them are things that nobody has successfully named yet.

SJL is almost two thousand years old. There has been plenty of time for weird shit to get into the walls, underneath the streets, inside the plumbing, behind somebody's house, and occasionally into somebody's family tree.

Monsters are not one species. They are not one culture. They are not all evil. They are not all supernatural. Some are predators. Some are pests. Some are basically wildlife. Some are intelligent enough to hold a conversation and still absolutely should not be invited inside your house.

BEHEMOTHS

BIG, WRONG, USEFUL.

Behemoths are their own thing. Huge, biologically inconsistent, physically impossible things that the city has spent centuries figuring out how to kill, butcher, process, build with, burn, sell, and occasionally fuck.

MONSTERS

THE OTHER SHIT.

Monsters are the rest of the problem. Some came from Behemoth biology. Some came from ordinary animals. Some came from whatever happens when reality has been sitting in one place for nineteen hundred years and nobody has cleaned up after it.

There is no master list.

There probably never will be.

DEMI-HUMANS

NOT MONSTERS.

Demi-humans are people.

They are part of the population. They have families, jobs, apartments, annoying neighbors, bad mornings, stupid tattoos, favorite restaurants, exes, cousins, rent, and all the other problems that come with being alive in SJL.

Their existence is tied to the long, extremely messy biological history between humans and Behemoths and monsters.

That does not make them monsters.

DEPENDS WHERE YOU ARE.

SJL does not have one universal monster problem. Every part of the city has its own flavor of bullshit.

03 / THINGS PEOPLE ALREADY KNOW

THERE AREN'T RULES.

There are just things people have learned the hard way.

01

If something copies your voice, don't answer it.

02

If the deer has the wrong number of joints, leave.

03

Do not buy a mattress from Mothball without checking inside it.

04

Do not assume a shadow belongs to the person standing in front of it.

05

If something in Nine is growing inside a carcass, somebody already knows.

06

Some things are intelligent. That doesn't mean they're friendly.

04 / LAST THING

YOU ARE GOING TO SEE SOMETHING WEIRD.

Everybody does eventually.

The trick is figuring out whether it wants to eat you, fuck you, follow you home, sell you something, or just stand there being weird.

Nobody has figured that part out yet.
