01

THE
NEIGHBORHOOD

The Hollows are old residential territory that was never properly abandoned.

Some houses are occupied. Some have been empty for years. Some are technically occupied according to municipal records even when nobody remembers seeing anybody come or go.

There are children here. There are grocery deliveries. People mow lawns. Somebody is always repairing a porch. Somebody always has a dog that will not stop barking.

It is still a neighborhood.

LOCAL NOTE

Do not assume an empty-looking house is empty.

02 / DEVELOPMENT

WHAT
HAPPENED
TO IT?

Nothing happened all at once.

That is the problem.

The Hollows became what they are through ordinary neglect. People moved. Properties changed hands. Buildings were divided up. Roads stopped getting repaired. Businesses closed. Families left houses behind.

The city grew around the neighborhood instead of replacing it.

Eventually there were more empty rooms than anybody could keep track of.

03

THE
HOUSES

You can't always tell which is which.

A maintained house can have nobody living inside it.

A collapsing house can have somebody cooking dinner upstairs.

A boarded window does not necessarily mean the building is abandoned.

BELOW

The Hollows were built on older things.

Roads. Foundations. Drainage. Utility tunnels. Rooms nobody finished.

The city did not remove them.

It built over them.
